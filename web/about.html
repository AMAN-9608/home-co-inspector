Who it’s for

A second look for inspectors. A clearer view for homebuyers.

Developed countries · e.g. USA

A co-inspector for professional home inspectors.

Keep spoken observations connected to specific video frames. Home Co-Inspector also proposes visible concerns that may not have been mentioned during the walkthrough. Inspectors review the evidence, edit descriptions, accept or reject findings, and sign off on the report.

Developing countries · e.g. India

A first-pass inspection aid for buyers and renters.

Our expansion vision: let buyers and renters record a smartphone walkthrough before handover or move-in and receive a structured list of visible concerns. They can review the findings and share them with a builder, landlord, or qualified professional.

How It Works

Reason. Locate. Check. Then let a person decide.

Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 work together in the backend pipeline: language and video reasoning guide visual localization, and the resulting evidence is checked again before review.

01

Muse Spark listens and looks

One pass extracts observations from the transcript. A separate video pass proposes visible issues that may not have been narrated. Both run in parallel.

02

SAM 3.1 locates the evidence

Defect and component phrases guide video segmentation. Tracked objects are aligned with the observation’s time window to produce localized evidence frames.

03

Muse Spark checks the frames

The model checks whether the observation is supported, whether it falls inside the box, and which frame shows it best.

04

The reviewer has the final word

Track overlap and semantic matching reduce duplicate findings. You review the evidence, add missing observations, and sign off on the report.
